What you can do

Evidence your auditors and customers can rely on

When a customer or auditor asks for evidence, the answers usually live in tickets, chat threads, and old scans. Rebuilding that story by hand takes days and still comes out incomplete. ZeroQuarry keeps the record as you work, so the report is already written.

01

Generate reports for customers and auditors

Build a customer-ready PDF from the assessments, fixes, and retests already on record: what was tested, what was found, and what happened next. Branding and disclaimers are applied for you, and a share link can expose only the findings a specific recipient needs.

02

Track disclosures to third parties

When you tell a customer, vendor, or coordinator about a vulnerability, ZeroQuarry keeps every milestone: reported, acknowledged, fixed, advisory published, credit given, closed. Months later you can still answer who was told what, and when.

03

Independent third-party signatures

Bring in an independent security partner to review and sign the report before it goes out, so customers and auditors see a signature from outside your team, not just your own word.

How it runs

Build, select, review, deliver

How a report or an Evidence Room export is assembled from work that already happened.

STEP 01

Build

Create evidence continuously through projects, scan versions, finding states, tickets, and retests.

STEP 02

Select

Choose the exact assets, reports, sections, findings, and confidence boundary relevant to the request.

STEP 03

Review

Remove irrelevant or sensitive context and confirm important findings have current decisions.

STEP 04

Deliver

Export a static pack or use a time-bounded share, then revoke access when the review ends.
