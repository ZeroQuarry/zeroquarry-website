What we scan

Reviewing code, artifacts, and runtimes together

Vulnerabilities found in source code can disappear when they go through your build system. Some vulnerabilities only show up based on deployment infrastructure that shows up during a live pentest. ZeroQuarry keeps all of these visible together.

01

Third-party dependencies

Your product runs on open-source packages you did not write. ZeroQuarry scans them for known vulnerabilities and for slopsquatting, where attackers publish malicious packages under the names AI coding assistants sometimes make up and wait for a developer to install one.

SAST + slopsquatting
02

Your source code

ZeroQuarry's AI agents read your code the way a security reviewer would, tracing authorization, data flow, input handling, secrets, and business logic across your repositories. Every reported finding includes the evidence that shows it is real.

AI agent review
03

Your release builds

Most teams ship through Docker, packaging scripts, or a build pipeline, and what goes in is not always what comes out. ZeroQuarry scans the artifact your customers actually receive: images, binaries, installers, and archives.

Artifact scan
04

Your deployment

Infrastructure-as-code decides what the internet can reach and what stays closed, and ZeroQuarry scans it. The software around your product, like API gateways, load balancers, and logging systems, can block vulnerabilities (by rejecting unauthorized access) or introduce new ones (path traversal, SSRF).

IaC scan
05

Your live product

Finally, ZeroQuarry's agents pentest the running product the way an attacker would. That shows which vulnerabilities survived all the way to something an attacker can reach, and which new ones appeared along the way.

Live pentest
How you use it

Scope, investigate, validate, act

Source, binary, and live testing follow the same four steps, and every step writes to the same project history.

STEP 01

Scope

Pick the product project and what to scan: a repository, a build, or a live app. Then add credentials and business context.

STEP 02

Investigate

ZeroQuarry's AI agents map your product and follow the attack paths that matter most.

STEP 03

Validate

Triage, proof generation, and optional vendor-style review pressure-test the claims.

STEP 04

Act

Route accepted findings into fixes, tickets, retests, disclosures, or evidence packs.

Evaluation FAQ

Before you point it at a real repository

Private repositories, live targets, and how this differs from a scanner.

Is ZeroQuarry a SAST or DAST scanner?

ZeroQuarry can do the things traditional SAST and DAST scanners do (scanning for vulnerabilities in code, your APIs, and websites) but can do much more. Its advanced team of AI agents can tie these different scan targets together and understand how they're built and where vulnerabilities may surface between each layer.

Can it test private repositories?

Yes. Accounts can configure scoped HTTPS or SSH Git credentials, and CI workflows can reference the saved credential without placing it in the scan payload.

Can ZeroQuarry test production?

Yes. ZeroQuarry can perform a penetration test on your production APIs, websites, and apps. Read more about how we keep agents contained in our agent containment post.
