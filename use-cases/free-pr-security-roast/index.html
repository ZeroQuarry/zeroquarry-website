Disclosure principles

Signal, not noise.

Every roast is specific, respectful, and actionable.

Specific to your code

Named vulnerability class. Exact line or branch. Real exploit path — not a warning about a theoretical pattern.

Respectful framing

"Here is something worth considering" — not "your code is insecure." We assume good faith and write accordingly.

No ZeroQuarry branding in the report

The finding stands on its own merit. A small footer notes it was produced by ZeroQuarry if you choose to share it publicly.

Always ask permission first

We will not make a roast public without your explicit consent. Your code, your call.

What we look for

What catches our eye in a PR

We focus on findings that are real, exploitable, and specific to the diff — not package vulnerabilities with no path to your code.

Auth and authorization gaps

Routes that check authentication but not ownership. Parameter tampering. IDOR in the making. Broken access control in new endpoints.

Injection paths

SQL, command, or LDAP injection where user input reaches a query or execution context. XSS where context matters.

Deserialization and serialization issues

Untrusted data that flows into a deserialization call. Config or payload parsing that assumes trust.

Auth bypass in new auth paths

New login, token, or session routes where a subtle logic error can bypass the check.

SSRF and open redirect

User-controlled URLs that reach internal services or enable phishing. Especially in webhooks and asset fetching.

Logic bugs with security impact

Race conditions, time-of-check-to-time-of-use gaps, state machine bugs, and subtle workflow bypasses.

FAQ

Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. No credit card, no trial, no sales call. One finding, sent directly to you, no strings attached.

What do you do with my code?

We analyze the diff and the context needed to understand the exploit path. We do not store your code longer than it takes to produce the finding. We do not share your code with third parties.

What if I do not agree with the finding?

You can dismiss it or reply with your reasoning. We may respond with additional context. The goal is to start a conversation, not to open a ticket in your tracker.

What if my PR is already merged by the time you review it?

Send us a note and we will close the request. Or let us know about a different open PR if you have one.

Why only OSS maintainers right now?

We are validating the format and the signal quality with a focused cohort first. Once we have a working playbook, we will expand to other audiences.

What is ZeroQuarry?

ZeroQuarry is an adversarial security validation platform. It finds real exploit paths in your code, produces bounded PoCs, scores findings by CVSS, and drafts patches — so you can separate noise from genuine risk. This roast program is a way to give back to OSS maintainers while testing the platform on real, diverse codebases.
